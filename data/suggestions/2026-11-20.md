### [high] wellness: host_duplicate
**Where:** 2 pages, e.g. http://shambhavewellness.org/, https://www.shambhavewellness.org/
**Problem:** http://shambhavewellness.org/ loads with 200 as well as https://shambhavewellness.org/. Pick one version and 301 the other.
**Fix:** Add a site-wide 301 in .htaccess: `RewriteCond %{HTTP_HOST} ^www\.(.+)$ [NC]` then `RewriteRule ^ https://%1%{REQUEST_URI} [L,R=301]` (and force https the same way).
